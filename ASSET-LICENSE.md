# Asset and content license

The root [MIT license](LICENSE) covers original source code, tests, build
tools, and ordinary project documentation unless a file or directory carries a
more specific notice.

It does not automatically cover game assets, recordings, fonts, names,
likenesses, branding, or authored content. Those materials keep the terms that
travel with them. Adding more art, audio, writing, or packs later does not
place that material under MIT unless its own notice says so.

## Original assets and writing

Original art, animation, audio, narrative writing, character depictions,
generated production sources, runtime files under `assets/`, and authored game
content remain **all rights reserved** except where a more specific license is
recorded with the file, directory, or content pack.

The built-in content pack states its own terms in
`src/omega_omarchy/data/content-packs/omega-core/LICENSE`. Community packs must
carry their own license and attribution. A pack's terms do not relicense the
engine or other packs.

## Separate licenses and future contributions

Third-party code, fonts, recordings, and marks are listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and in notices shipped beside
those files. Their upstream licenses govern; they are not relicensed by the
root MIT license.

When a contribution needs different terms—now or later—record them with the
contribution: a file- or directory-level notice, a pack license, or an update
to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Until that notice exists, do not treat the
material as MIT-licensed.
