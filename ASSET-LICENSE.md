# Asset and content license status

This file narrows the scope of the root MIT license. It is a provisional
private-alpha classification, not the final rights-clearance record required
for a public release.

## MIT-licensed material

The root `LICENSE` applies to original source code, tests, build tools, and
ordinary project documentation unless a file or directory carries a more
specific notice.

## Original game assets and writing

Original art, animation, audio, narrative writing, character depictions, and
generated production sources are currently **all rights reserved** pending the
pre-public provenance and rights review. No redistribution license is granted
for these materials by the root MIT license. This provisional scope includes
the runtime files under `assets/`, except for third-party branding discussed
below and the third-party typeface discussed next, and the authored game
content embedded in the core pack.

The built-in content pack has its own private-alpha notice at
`src/omega_omarchy/data/content-packs/omega-core/LICENSE`. Community packs must
carry their own license and attribution; a pack's terms do not relicense the
engine or other packs.

The maintainer-supplied *Make It Come Alive* recording, its decoded FLAC
preservation master, and every derived runtime tier are included in this
all-rights-reserved private-alpha category. Their container/master digests and
provisional provenance are recorded in `assets/source/audio/`. Permission for
public source or binary redistribution has not yet been established and is a
blocking public-cutover review item.

## Third-party typeface

The vendored Omarchy Font files under `assets/source/fonts/` and their runtime
copies under `assets/ui/` remain under Mark Cuda's upstream MIT License. The
complete text, pinned revision, and attribution are recorded in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. These files are excluded from the original-asset
reservation above.

## Names, likenesses, and personas

No permission to use a real person's name, likeness, voice, image, signature,
persona, or publicity rights is granted by the MIT license or this file. The
David character and any other recognizable depiction require a separate,
documented rights decision before public cutover.

Private visual-reference photographs are not tracked or packaged. Their
absence is not itself proof that every resulting depiction is cleared.

## Trademarks and third-party branding

Omarchy and related names, logos, and marks belong to their respective owners.
The source and treatment of retained Omarchy/Oligarchy geometry are documented
under `assets/source/branding/README.md`. Those files and derived in-game marks
are not offered under the root MIT license. No trademark license, affiliation,
sponsorship, authorization, or endorsement is claimed.

## Before public distribution

The founder must approve a file-by-file provenance inventory and either assign
an explicit original-asset license, obtain required permissions, replace the
material, or exclude it. Until that review is committed, this repository and
its native bundles remain private technical-alpha material and must not be
treated as redistributable open-source releases.
