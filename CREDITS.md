## Inspirations and references

Omega Omarchy is an unofficial parody/fan project inspired by user-owned
computing, Linux culture, Omarchy, and Oligarchy. Omarchy/Oligarchy name and logo
provenance is documented in `assets/source/branding/README.md`; their respective
owners do not sponsor or endorse this project.

## Runtime and tooling

Python, pygame-ce, Pillow, NumPy, qrcode, and PyInstaller make the native game
and artifact possible. Exact versions, license summaries, and copied license

`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
