# Governance

Omega Omarchy is a founder-led community project in preparation. The lead
maintainer is the final decision-maker for product direction, canon, voice,
architecture, releases, repository visibility, security disclosure, and merge
authority.

Contributors may propose, critique, implement, test, document, and maintain
work. Maintainers evaluate proposals against the design, current roadmap,
player evidence, safety, rights, accessibility, performance, and long-term
coherence. Participation does not create a vote on canon or guarantee merge,
credit placement, roadmap inclusion, or release timing.

Routine reversible implementation choices may be delegated. Changes to canon,
central satire/voice, compatibility contracts, licensing, contributor policy,
security posture, supported platforms, or public claims require explicit lead
maintainer approval. Important decisions should be recorded in an issue, pull
request, or design document with alternatives and evidence.

The maintainer may close discussions that repeat settled decisions, expand
scope without evidence, solicit unauthorized public release, or turn targeted
collaboration into design-by-committee. Disagreement is welcome when it remains
specific, respectful, and evidence-led.

Repository visibility remains private until the separate cutover procedure in
`docs/OPEN-SOURCE-CUTOVER.md` passes and the founder authorizes an exact commit.
