# Omega Omarchy roadmap

This is a small directional roadmap, not a promise or community vote. The lead
maintainer owns ordering, scope, canon, and release decisions.

## Current checkpoint

Chapter 1 is playable and automatically traversed end to end, the deterministic
generator is at `3.8.0`, and the native artifact/contribution foundations are
credible. The 2026-09-03 complete local gate passed 298 tests with one expected
skip; the authoritative Chapter 1 browser alpha also reaches the installer and
accepts keyboard input in desktop Chromium. This is still a technical alpha:
preview feedback should concentrate on fun, clarity, pacing,
movement, combat, and first-run friction rather than treating later chapters
as complete.

## Accepted: 0.2 Open Development Alpha

- Polish and human-qualify a coherent 10–15 minute Chapter 1.
- Ship and clean-environment-test the portable native Linux artifact.
- Complete the contribution, security, rights, history-audit, and public-claim
  surfaces required by the cutover gates.
- Expand the safe data-only content-pack contract beyond generation profiles.
- Demonstrate one genuine two-installation Limitless publish/discover/apply
  exchange with local verification and rollback.
- Produce curated release screenshots and a short honest gameplay trailer.

## Accepted direction: 0.2.x

- Accessibility, performance, tooling, pack-ecosystem growth, and Chapter 1
  refinement/content breadth.
- Independent audio fidelity, an authored three-tier Chapter 1 soundtrack, and
  the cue/manifest pipeline defined in `docs/sound-support.md`.

## Accepted direction: 0.3+

- End-to-end production and qualification of Chapters 2–6 in deliberate slices.
- Ghost/replay viewing, broader platform work, and additional asynchronous
  sharing as each surface becomes production-ready.
- Omarchy native-shell panel qualification on supported hardware.

## Exploratory, not committed

- A Godot presentation migration, only if measured export, shader, controller,
  startup, memory, and maintenance results beat the pygame-ce target.
- Full-campaign web parity and broader browser/mobile qualification.
- Synchronous multiplayer and broader network services.
- Photo-derived character creation after privacy and rights design.

## Out of scope for 0.2

- Claiming all six chapters are complete.
- Design-by-committee control of voice or canon.
- Arbitrary executable mods or agent access to the host filesystem.
- Mandatory accounts, network access, Limitless, telemetry, or silent sharing.
- A public repository cutover without the founder's exact-commit approval.
